About the unit

The Seismic Hazard Resilience Unit (SHRU) was established in 2025 at the College of Engineering, University of Duhok. It brings together structural, geotechnical and earthquake engineers to assess seismic hazard, evaluate the vulnerability of existing buildings, and support the safe design of new construction.

Research

Hazard, vulnerability and risk studies grounded in regional data.

Education & training

Seminars, workshops and graduate supervision on seismic design and assessment.

Engineering support

Technical guidance for public bodies, designers and the community.
